This policy explains what data Elim Sende (Android, iOS and PC), developed by Semore Games, collects, why, and your rights. The data controller is Semore Games. Contact: semoregames@gmail.com

Data we collect

DataPurpose
Player name (chosen by you)Shown to other players in online games, leaderboards and friend lists.
Account identifier: a random ID generated on your device; if you sign in, your Google Play Games player ID (Android), Apple user ID (iOS, "Sign in with Apple") or Steam ID (PC)To link your progress to your account, let you continue on another device and prevent cheating. With Sign in with Apple we do not request your name or email.
Game data: points, unlocked characters, upgrades, leaderboard scores, friends list and requests, players you blockedTo run the game; stored on our server linked to your account.
Purchase records: pack, order / transaction ID, dateTo add purchased points to your account and prevent the same purchase being counted twice. Payments are processed by Google Play, the App Store or Steam; we never see your card details.
Reports: the report you send, reason, your comment, your IP address at that time, game version and OSTo review abusive behaviour and prevent misuse.
Technical data: IP address, game version, OS nameTo connect, check version compatibility and keep server logs (debugging and security).
In-match movement dataSent in real time to play online matches; not stored.

On Android, if you sign in to Play Games, a backup of your progress is kept in your Google account (Play Games saved games), stored by Google.

The game contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools. We do not use your data for advertising, sell it or share it with others. If this changes we will update this policy.

Who we share data with

  • Hosting: Hetzner Online GmbH (European Union), where the game server and its records are hosted.
  • Google (Play Games sign-in, Play Billing), Apple (Sign in with Apple, App Store payments), Valve (Steam sign-in and payments): only to verify sign-ins and payments.
  • Other players: only your player name, badges, leaderboard score and online status are visible.
  • Authorities, where required by law.

Retention

  • Account and game data: until you delete your account.
  • Server logs (including IP addresses): up to 30 days.
  • Purchase records: as long as required by law; your account ID is removed from them when you delete your account.
  • Reports: up to 12 months for review; the reporter's ID is removed when their account is deleted.

Deleting your account

In the game: Settings → Account & Privacy → Delete Account. Details: account deletion page. If you can't access the game, email semoregames@gmail.com with your player name.

Security

Sign-ins and purchases are verified on our server directly with Google, Apple and Valve; the game only forwards the proof. Only authorised people can access the server.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe we have such data, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights (GDPR and Turkish KVKK)

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to object to processing and to data portability. Send requests to semoregames@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

We may update this policy and will announce important changes in the game. The current version is always at this address.
